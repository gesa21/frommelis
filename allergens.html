Allergens and ingredients

What is on every tub

Every tub carries a full ingredient list, with allergens printed in bold as UK law requires. It also carries the date the dish was made, and storage and reheating instructions for that dish.

The menu cards on the main page list the allergens in each dish this week. If you or your children have an allergy or intolerance, message us on WhatsApp before you order and we will talk you through exactly what is in each dish.

The 14 allergens we label

Celery, cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, lupin, milk, molluscs, mustard, peanuts, sesame, soya, sulphur dioxide and sulphites, and tree nuts.

Everything is cooked in 1 kitchen. We handle allergens carefully and clean thoroughly between dishes, but we cannot call any dish free of a given allergen.

How we cook and freeze

Everything is cooked at the weekend in Twickenham by chef Olsi. Each dish is portioned, labelled and frozen within 2 hours of the pan, because freezing is our only preservative. There are no additives and nothing you would not use at home.

Storage and reheating

Keep everything frozen at minus 18 degrees until you want it. Defrost overnight in the fridge, or cook straight from frozen where the tub says so. Always reheat until piping hot all the way through. Once a dish has been defrosted, eat it within 24 hours and do not refreeze it.
