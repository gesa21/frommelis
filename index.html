Questions

Is frozen actually fresh?

Cooked Saturday. Frozen Saturday. Nothing added. Every dish is frozen within 2 hours of the pan, which holds it at its best. Freezing is our only preservative, so there is nothing in the tub except what Olsi cooked.

Do I have to commit to anything?

No sign-up. No minimum. Order when you want a week off. Order 1 week, skip 3, come back when the diary fills up. Nothing renews.

What if my kids will not eat it?

Every adult main comes as a kids portion, and the 3 dedicated kids dishes carry 5 vegetables blended smooth into sauces that look completely plain. If you are not sure what yours will go for, message us on WhatsApp and we will help you choose.

Where do you deliver?

Twickenham, St Margarets, Strawberry Hill, Teddington, Whitton, Richmond, North Sheen, Kew, East Sheen, Mortlake, Ham, Petersham, Isleworth, Hampton, Hampton Hill, Hampton Wick and Kingston. Somewhere else nearby, ask on WhatsApp.
